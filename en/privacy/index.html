Privacy

Draft, written from the actual technical implementation. To be reviewed by legal counsel and completed with the controller's details before the site is released.

Controller

Real North AG as the client of the Im Birkenhain project.

To be added: address and an email address for data subjects to contact.

What data is processed

Only what you enter in the registration form yourself: first name, last name and email address are required; phone number, preferred number of rooms, minimum floor area, move-in date, parking space, further wishes (garden apartment, penthouse apartment, hobby room, pet) and notes are optional. No personal data is collected if you do not register.

Purpose

The details are used solely to add you to the registration list and to contact you when letting begins. No profiling and no automated individual decision-making takes place.

Spam protection

Your registration is stored when you submit the form; there is no confirmation email. To keep the form from being abused for bulk messages, the server checks every submission against several signals: a field invisible to people that must stay empty, how long the form took to fill in, where it was submitted from, links in fields that should not contain any, and whether the domain of your email address accepts mail at all. No captcha and no third-party service is used, so no data is passed to outside servers. Submissions sorted out this way are kept for 90 days, so that a wrong call can be noticed and corrected, and deleted afterwards.

Retention

Registrations are kept until initial letting is complete and are deleted afterwards. You may request deletion at any time; registering again remains possible.

Security and storage location

The data is held on the hosting provider's web server in a file outside the publicly accessible area. Your email address is additionally stored as a check value (HMAC) so that duplicate registrations can be detected without keeping the address as a plaintext lookup key. Only the client can view the list, through a password-protected address that is not linked anywhere and is blocked for search engines.

Abuse protection

To prevent automated mass registrations, the number of attempts per IP address and per email address is counted for at most one hour. The IP address is used only as a counter, is not linked to your registration, and is deleted once the hour has passed.

Disclosure to third parties

No data is disclosed for advertising purposes and no data is sold. The hosting provider's mail server is used to send the internal notification and acts as a processor in that respect.

To be added: name and domicile of the hosting provider.

Map

The map on «Location & mobility» loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap. It loads automatically with the page; this transmits your IP address to OpenStreetMap. No cookies are set for this, and nothing is stored in your browser.

Cookies, tracking, fonts

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and embeds no advertising networks. Fonts are served from our own domain, not by a third party. The web server logs access technically, as is usual with any web hosting.

To be added: retention period of the hosting provider's server access logs.

Your rights

You have the right to information about the data processed about you, to rectification, to erasure and to data portability. An email to the address above is sufficient. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

First letting follows — be on the interest list before it does.
