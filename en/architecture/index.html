Architecture

Seventeen town houses, stepped towards the forest

Multi-storey volumes are built together and staggered in height so that density is barely perceptible. Recessed plinths, shifted façades and passages at ground level break the volumes into readable units.

Compact volumes with punched façades: windows and doors as clearly defined openings in a homogeneous wall. Upper floors cantilever at least 80 cm over the plinth on the main living sides.

The fifth façade counts too: flat roofs become planted roof gardens wherever no photovoltaics are installed.
