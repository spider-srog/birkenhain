A grove at the centre

A beautiful way to live

Seventeen staggered town houses gather around a generous neighbourhood square, planted with the birch grove that gives the quarter its name. Every home looks into greenery: the forest edge, the courtyards, the planted roof gardens.

Between the forest and the existing village, a quarter with its own identity emerges — dense, but small-grained; ambitious, but quiet.

The location

At the end of the road, ten minutes from the train

Rudolfstetten-Friedlisberg sits on the Mutschellen at 550 m, between the Reuss valley and the Limmat valley. The site is the last one before the Isleren forest — and still inside the well-served centre.
