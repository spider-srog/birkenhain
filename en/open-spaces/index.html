Open spaces & landscape

The forest continues into the quarter

The landscape design continues the layering of the forest: large trees, shrubs, herbaceous and ground layers. That is the contrast to the architecture — and what makes the courtyards feel cool in summer.

Along the forest edge, meeting points and play areas for different ages: a volleyball court, a forest playground, a bootcamp, a boccia lane. Two further playgrounds sit inside the quarter.

The path network

Like the roots of a tree

The paths branch from the forest edge down through the quarter and out to the surrounding neighbourhoods — wide where people meet, narrow where they simply pass. Nothing ends in a dead end.

And where the paths meet, a signature tree marks the difference: the vegetation of the quarter is read as its own layer, distinct from the forest around it.

The signature tree

White trunks against the dark forest

The birch is the tree that tells the quarter apart from the woodland behind it: pale trunks, a light canopy, moving shade. Planted as a grove on the square and as a gateway at the Alpenweg entrance, it turns the name into something you can see.
