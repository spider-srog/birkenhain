Apartments · first letting

300 apartments to rent, 2.5 to 5.5 rooms

All apartments are for rent — none are for sale. The mix runs from the 2.5-room flat to the 5.5-room family apartment, with the three-and-a-half and four-and-a-half-room flats forming the core. Layouts and areas are provisional; rents and the letting schedule follow later.

Living, dining and kitchen in one room, with the loggia to the west. Visualisation, non-binding.

Built for three ways of living

The building code carries the target group

The apartments are laid out for three groups of residents, and each building is assigned to one of them — the letter in the building code says which.

D DINKs

Double income, no kids

Compact, well-cut flats for couples who are out a lot: open living rooms, a generous loggia, the bistro and the gym a minute away.

All buildings marked D

F Families

Families

The larger 4.5 and 5.5-room flats, close to the day-care, the playgrounds and the square — and the whole estate is car-free above ground.

All buildings marked F

B Best agers 50+

Best agers, 50 plus

Step-free flats in the lower row houses along Obere Dorfstrasse: shops within walking distance, the forest at the door, quiet.

All buildings marked B

Inside

  • Oak floors in the living rooms, hard-wearing surfaces in the cooking zone.

  • Underfloor heating with room-by-room control — 21 °C living, 23 °C bathrooms — and passive cooling through the same circuit in summer.

  • Triple glazing and deep window reveals in the punched façade; windows that open, no mechanical ventilation.

  • Balconies at least 80 cm deep on the main living side; a roof terrace on the top floors.
