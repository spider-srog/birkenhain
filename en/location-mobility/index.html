Location & mobility

Between the Mutschellen junction and the forest

The site lies on the edge of the Mutschellen junction — well connected, yet at the end of the road. The S17 runs every 15 minutes to Dietikon and on to Zürich; two stops are within ten minutes on foot.

Cars go underground right at the entrance to the site; above ground the estate stays car-free, with access only for deliveries, emergency vehicles and moving days.

  • Berikon-Widen and Hofacker stations 500 m · 10 min
  • S17 to Dietikon / Zürich every 15 min
  • Zürich HB from Berikon-Widen 31 min
  • Shops, Mutschellen centre walking distance
  • Isleren forest directly adjacent
  • Dietikon / Bremgarten by bike 20–30 min

Cycling & walking

Above ground the estate is car-free — and the centre stays free of bicycle traffic too. Bikes are parked at the edge of the development and in the basement.

Centre

Traffic-free

The centre stays free of cars and bicycles. The paths inside belong to people on foot.

At the edge

Covered bike stands

Enough covered bike parking is planned along the edge of the development — close to the entrances and out of the weather.

Basement

Room for trailers too

Generous bike parking is planned below ground, with space for trailers.

E-bikes

Power and lockable cabinets

E-bike spaces are planned with a power supply, plus lockable cabinets for helmet, charger and the like.
